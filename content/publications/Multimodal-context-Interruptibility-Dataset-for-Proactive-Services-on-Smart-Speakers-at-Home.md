abstract: |
  As smart speakers become increasingly integrated into everyday home life, there is growing interest in using them to proactively deliver personalized services. This work presents INPROSH, the first publicly available interruptibility dataset focused on home environments. Collected from 26 participants during a three-week in-the-wild field study, INPROSH contains 2,830 cases annotated with interruptibility labels and enriched with temporal, spatial, and behavioral context, as well as surrounding image and sound recordings near smart speakers. The dataset supports research into understanding and detecting opportune moments for proactive services in the home.
